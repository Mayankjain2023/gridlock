Privacy Policy — GRIDLOCK Block Puzzle

Last updated: March 2026

GRIDLOCK ("the app") is a free mobile puzzle game developed by Veltrix Games. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

GRIDLOCK does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app works fully offline.

The only data stored is your game progress (high score, adventure level stars) saved locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

GRIDLOCK does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

GRIDLOCK is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
