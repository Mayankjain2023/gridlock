GRIDLOCK — Block Puzzle Game

Drop blocks. Clear lines. Chase the high score.

GRIDLOCK is a beautifully designed block puzzle game that's easy to learn but impossible to put down. Place colorful pieces on the board, clear full rows and columns, and keep the grid clean as long as you can.

• Beautiful warm color palette, easy on the eyes

★ HOW TO PLAY
1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board
2. Fill a complete row or column to clear it
3. Clearing multiple lines at once scores a COMBO bonus
4. Game ends when no pieces can be placed
